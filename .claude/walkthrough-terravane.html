Getting started

From a cold machine to signed in

Everything between double-clicking the launcher and landing on your dashboard — the local stack, the welcome screen, and choosing who you are on the network.

1

Start the local stack

Double-click start-terravane.bat in the project's root folder. No commands to type.

It compiles the contracts, boots a local chain, deploys them, seeds a season of demo produce, and starts the server — two console windows will open and stay open while it runs.

What you'll see "Terravane Chain" and "Terravane Server" windows, each filling with log lines. Leave both open — closing either one takes the app down.
2

Open the site

Your browser opens automatically once the server's ready. If it doesn't, go to http://localhost:4300 yourself.

3

The welcome screen

You'll land on a live snapshot of the network — lots on the ledger, blocks indexed, participants enrolled — before you've chosen anything. It's reading straight off the chain, not a mockup.

4

Pick your role

Farmer, Processor, Distributor, Retailer, Certifier, Inspector, Sensor gateway, or Admin. This decides what the console lets you do, not who you're allowed to pretend to be — every role here is a real enrolled participant type on the chain.

5

Pick your organisation

Choosing a role narrows the list to organisations actually enrolled under it. For Farmer, that's:

Sundar Farms Nilgiri Highland Estates Konkan Mango Co-op Anand Growers Collective
No password, on purpose There's no login form because there's nothing to type. Your identity here is a chain address — picking the org signs you in as it, using local development keys this build holds for you.
You're in

Dashboard, live

One click after picking your org, you're looking at real seeded data — held lots, pending signatures, a recalled tea lot, a cold-chain breach on a mango shipment. Everything from here is a separate walkthrough.
