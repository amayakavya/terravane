Lot page

Everyone

Every lot's full record, six tabs: Overview (who grew it, held by, cold chain range), Route (custody trail on a map), Timeline, Lineage (splits and merges as a graph), Cold chain (every temperature reading, excursions in red), and Actions.

Actions tab — handover

Custodian ↔ recipient

Custody never moves in one step. Whoever's holding a lot proposes a transfer; the header shows Acting as: [your org] the whole time so it's never ambiguous whose signature this is.

1 Holder picks Propose handover, names the recipient, signs.
2 Lot shows "Awaiting acceptance by [recipient]" — a visible custody gap, not a silent limbo.
3 Recipient signs in, opens the same lot's Actions tab, sees Accept handover waiting.
4 One signature later: "Held by" flips, the gap clears, verdict goes back to Verified.

Actions tab — everything else

Role-gated

The same tab surfaces different actions depending who's signed in: advance stage, record sensor reading, certify, inspect, split / merge, sell, recall, destroy — only the ones your role and your custody actually permit.

Recall Flagging a lot's root propagates automatically through every split or merged descendant it ever became — you never chase down child lots by hand.

Oversight & the public record

What a regulator sees network-wide, and what a shopper sees with no account at all.

Regulator view

Admin

Network-wide, not lot-by-lot: recalls grouped by region, cold-chain breaches grouped by region. The view a regulator actually needs — where the problems are, not who has which lot.

Public trace + printable label

No sign-in

Every lot has a QR code. Scanning it — or opening Public trace from any lot page — opens a page with no login: a verdict banner (Verified / Caution / Unsafe) up top, warnings underneath if any, then provenance, certifications, and the full journey.

The Printable label is the same QR code plus lot number, origin, and storage window, laid out for a physical pack.
